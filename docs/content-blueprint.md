# CONFIGUSA
## Website Content Blueprint
### Page-by-page website copy and content direction
A premium, Big-4-inspired enterprise positioning for technology, engineering, workforce and GCC solutions.

---

### THE POSITIONING DIRECTION
**Technology. Talent. Transformation.**
The right expertise to solve complex technology and workforce challenges.

#### How to use this document
- Use the copy as a content foundation, then validate every service, credential, statistic and client reference before publishing.
- Replace bracketed placeholders such as [verified number] and [approved client example] with approved company information.
- Keep each page focused on one buyer need. Move detailed explanations, long lists and market reports into relevant inner pages or Insights.
- Do not claim official partnerships, certifications, client relationships, implementation experience or measurable outcomes unless ConfigUSA can substantiate them.
- Keep the existing ConfigUSA logo and approved brand identity. The proposed visual direction is deep navy, corporate blue, white/light grey and restrained red.

### 9. Workforce Solutions & Engagement Models
**PAGE PURPOSE:** Help buyers understand the commercial models available and choose the right one.

**Hero section**
- Headline: Flexible workforce models. Clear delivery expectations.
- Supporting copy: Different business needs require different workforce models. ConfigUSA works with clients to align the engagement structure to role criticality, duration, hiring urgency and delivery ownership.
- Primary CTA: Select an Engagement Model

- Contract Staffing: Access specialist professionals for defined roles and durations. Suitable for project demand, temporary capacity needs and specialist skill gaps.
- Contract-to-Hire: Engage a professional for an agreed evaluation period, with the option to consider permanent employment subject to the agreed commercial and legal terms.
- Direct Placement: Recruit permanent professionals for specialist, managerial and leadership positions through a structured search and selection process.
- Managed Workforce / Delivery: Mobilise and coordinate a defined group of professionals against agreed scope, service expectations, governance and reporting. The exact delivery accountability must be stated in the contract.

**How to choose**
We consider the required skills, duration, urgency, location, supervision model, compliance requirements and desired outcome before recommending an engagement structure.

**Publishing notes**
- Keep commercial terms, replacement periods, guarantees and service levels in proposals or contracts—not generic website copy.
- Use consistent naming across navigation, service pages and enquiry forms.

### Final Content and Publishing Checklist
- One clear purpose and one primary CTA on every page.
- No repeated paragraphs or duplicate statistics across pages.
- All company history, headcount, deployment, client and performance claims verified.
- All client names, logos, testimonials and case studies approved for public use.
- All partner, certification and platform claims substantiated.
- Each service page explains the client problem, ConfigUSA response, delivery model and evidence.
- Short paragraphs, meaningful headings, concise cards and useful internal links.
- Consistent page titles, meta descriptions, image alt text and heading hierarchy.
- Forms, email links, phone links, downloads and mobile navigation tested.
- Desktop, tablet and mobile versions reviewed before production release.
